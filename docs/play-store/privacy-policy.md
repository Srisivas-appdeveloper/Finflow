# FinFlow privacy policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

This policy explains how the FinFlow app ("FinFlow", "the app"), published
by **Kalainova**, handles your information.

## Summary

FinFlow stores everything you enter only on your device. We do not collect,
receive, sell or share any personal or financial data. The app has no
servers, no user accounts and no internet access.

## Information you enter

You can enter transactions (amounts, payees, categories, dates, notes),
credit card details (a nickname, issuer name, credit limit, billing days and
optionally the last four digits of the card number), bill and instalment
(EMI) details, and split groups (a group name, the first names or nicknames
you type for the people in it, and the shared expenses and repayments you
record). FinFlow never asks for, and you should never enter, a full
card number, CVV, PIN or banking password.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It
is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

## Information we collect

None. FinFlow does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or
tracking software, and it does not request internet access. FinFlow does not
read your SMS messages, emails, notifications, contacts or location.

## Notifications

If you turn on bill reminders, FinFlow asks for permission to show
notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally by Android; their content
never leaves your device.

## Backups and export

FinFlow excludes its data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device
transfer, so your financial records are not copied to a cloud account.
You can export your transactions as a CSV file using the app's Export
option, and share a text summary of a split group's balances. Both happen
only when you tap them; you choose where the file or text goes (for example
a messaging app, an email or a drive), and its handling is then governed by
the app or service you choose.

## Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time in the app under Profile > Your data >
Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data from your
device.

## Children

FinFlow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Because
FinFlow does not collect data, changes cannot affect information we hold
about you.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: contact Kalainova using the developer email
shown on FinFlow's Google Play store page.
